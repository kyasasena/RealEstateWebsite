About Kya

Where Strategy Meets Heart

REALTOR® · Keller Williams Realty Buckhead

I'm Kya Sasena, a REALTOR® with Keller Williams Realty Buckhead and my path to real estate is anything but typical. Before helping clients buy and sell homes, I spent years in data analytics and animal rescue. That combination might sound unexpected, but it shaped exactly the kind of agent I am today.

I approach every transaction the way I approached data: methodically, patiently, and with a genuine investment in the outcome. I translate complex market information into clear, confident decisions so you always know where you stand and why.

But numbers only tell part of the story. What drives me is the people. Whether you're buying your first home, selling a place full of memories, or navigating a tough market, I show up with the same care and precision every time.
